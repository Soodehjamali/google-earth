[S1] Hokmabadi H. (2010) CIHEAM. Pistachio frost damage in Iran and new methods of frost protection. Options Mediterraneennes A94, pp. 71-78. https://om.ciheam.org/om/pdf/a94/00801286.pdf
 - Bloomed flowers: critical temperature about -1.7 C (p. 77). Air vs tissue temperature is NOT stated; loggers were beside flower buds, 1 m above ground.
 - Flower buds: about -4 C for about 2 h => light injury (delayed bloom) and, in another sentence, complete necrosis of flowers/inflorescence (the paper is ambiguous); -4 to -6 C for about 6 h => necrosis of very young pistils (p. 72). No 10/50/90 % damage levels are given.
 - Weed-free, firm, moist soil can add 1 to 1.7 C protection in radiation frost (p. 72). The paper CITES this from Snyder & Connell (1993); it is NOT a pistachio measurement.
 - Radiation frost = clear skies, calm wind, temperature inversion (p. 72).
 - Author's own measurements: fog machine about +1.6 to +1.9 C (text and abstract disagree), SIS about +2 C average, heaters (65/ha) at least +2 C, smoke little benefit. The paper contains a unit error ("120 km/h" fan wind); use with care.
[S2] Hokmabadi H. (10 Apr 2011, 21 Farvardin 1390) Iran Pistachio Association. Spring frost (سرمازدگی بهاره). https://www.iranpistachio.org/fa/site-map/baqbani/625-bahare%22
 - Critical temperatures: flower +2 C, opening bud -2 C, unopened bud -4 C, fruit -2 C; swelling bud has no number. Air vs tissue not stated. The flower value (+2) CONFLICTS with S1 (-1.7). No duration given.
 - Radiation frost: clear, calm. Advective frost: mild-to-strong cold wind, often cloudy, may last several nights.
 - Soil: moist, smooth, weed-free soil protects; wetting darkens soil and increases absorption; light soils warm quickly but are damaged more in frost; avoid tillage in frost-prone periods.
 - Other factors: site, slope aspect, relief; cultivar (early-bloom Ouhadi and Kalleh-Ghouchi more exposed than late-bloom Akbari); tree age (very young/old more sensitive); pests/diseases.
 - Protection: heaters costly and NOT recommended; smoke has environmental limits; sprinklers, wind machines, inversion wells (SIS), fog machines (up to about 4 C).
